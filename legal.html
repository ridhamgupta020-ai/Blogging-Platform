Disclosures

Privacy

Account information and articles are stored in the platform's SQLite database. Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes. Authentication uses an HTTP-only, same-site session cookie. Uploaded images are stored on the server. Newsletter subscriptions are stored so the platform can manage its subscriber list.

This project does not currently send email campaigns or use third-party analytics. Newsletter signup stores your email but does not send a confirmation or marketing email.

Service terms

Keep your account credentials private and only publish content you have the right to share. You are responsible for the content you submit. Account owners can update or delete their own articles and comments.

This self-hosted application is provided as-is. Operators should configure HTTPS, a strong session secret, backups, and appropriate access controls before using it with real users.

Affiliate disclosure

Some editorial pages may discuss products or services. This demo platform does not process affiliate links or payments. Any commercial relationship should be clearly disclosed by the author in the relevant article.
